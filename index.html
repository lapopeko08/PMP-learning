1.3.3 Operations and Project management

Operation focus on efficient and effective production of products and/or services; manage processes to transform input -> output

Ongoing operation are outside of scope of a project. However, there are intersecting points : when developing new products/services; improving product; end of product lifecycle and closeout phase or iteration

Section 2. A System for Value Delivery

2.1 Creating value

Business value is a net quantifiable benefit, categorized into tangible and intangible

Tangible: Monetary assests, Productivity, Infrastructure improvements, etc.

Intangible: Brand recognition & trademark, public benefit, reputation

Value Delivery System

Component: Vision > Mission > Organizational Strategy & Objective > Portfolio > Programs > Project & Operation

Connection: Each component produce outcome. Outcome produce benefit (or disbenefit). Benefit creates value.

Project success

Dimension 1: Project outcome – how effectiveness project realizes value.

Dimension 2: Project management processes – how well project adhere to constraints (cost, scope, time, quality)

2.2 Project Environment

Two categories of influences: EEFs – enterprise environmental factors and OPAs – organizational process assets.

EEF External Marketplace conditions, social influences, Regulations, etc.
Internal Organizational cuture, Geographic distribution, Resource capability, Financial capability
OPA Process, Policies, Procedures
Knowledge Repo

2.3 Product management 

Integrate of people, data, processes, business system to create, develop, maintain a product or a service. 

PdM - provide vision and strategy for product 

PgM / PjM - ensure the vision is brought to life by managing execution, dependencies & resources. structuring & guiding the development process, risk, ensure alignment with organizational 

PO - ensure team focus on delivering valuable features by managing/prioritizing backlog 

BA - gather and document requirements, ensure project scope align biz needs


2.5 Project Management Roles

Bringing focus & clarity to the work 

Manage needs and task effectively 

Prioritize & allocate attention 

Competencies

 - Social responsibilities: project aligned common good 

- Power skills: critical thinking, decision making, imp team motivation methods, negotiation & conflict resolution 

- Business acumen: skill in making correct decision by understanding the business 

- Ways of working: appropriate project management approach, brainstorm techniques

- Results: ability to deal with enterprise politics

Accountable Leader

Projects may involve multiple departments, vendor, org, that do not interact on regular basis; carry higher stakes and expectations than regular operational functions. => Broader array of stakeholders attempt to influence project. Diversity of influence cause confusion and conflict.

So, accountable leadership = responsible & own project target business objectives, actions taken, decisions made. 

- Influence member

- Accountable for member actions 

- Effectively lead by examples

- Effectively adapt style 

- Foster an environment of safety, collaboration
